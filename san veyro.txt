==================================================

Title: BLOOD & MONEY

Main character: Alex Carter.

Alex returns to San Veyro after seven years away.

His older brother Daniel disappeared three months earlier.

Alex wants to discover what happened.

He reconnects with his childhood friend Marcus Reed.

Marcus helps him get back into city life.

Alex meets Sophia Miller, an investigator who is also looking into Daniel's disappearance.

Their investigation leads them to Victor Hayes, a powerful businessman who controls important parts of San Veyro.

The deeper Alex investigates, the more secrets he discovers.

- 25-35 main missions.
- 30+ side missions.
- Character development.
- Unexpected plot twists.
- Friendships.
- Betrayals.
- Cinematic storytelling.
- Dialogue choices.
- Meaningful decisions.
- Multiple endings.
